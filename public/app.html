Transparency & Methodology

How QEI Scores Are Calculated

Every score, estimate, and risk value on this site comes from a transparent, open model. This page explains exactly how — including where we use real data, where we make estimates, and where AI is involved.

The Risk Score Model

Each entity receives a score from 0–100 based on five weighted factors. Scores are computed entirely in your browser — no server call required for indexed entities.

Market / Cap Weight×25%
User Exposure×20%
Crypto Vulnerability×30%
Migration Difficulty×15%
Time to Migrate×10%
= Risk Score (0–100)
Factor Definitions
FactorWeightWhat it measures
Market / Cap Weight25%How much capital is at risk relative to the entity's market cap or total assets. Larger = higher systemic impact.
User Exposure20%How many users or institutions are directly affected by a quantum breach. Custodial services score higher than self-custody protocols.
Crypto Vulnerability30%How directly the entity's cryptographic scheme is broken by Shor's algorithm. ECDSA, RSA, and Ed25519 score high. NIST PQC-native schemes score near zero.
Migration Difficulty15%How hard it is to move to post-quantum cryptography. Decentralised protocols with no upgrade authority score highest. Centralised custodians score lower.
Time to Migrate10%Estimated years needed to complete a full migration given current trajectory. Longer timelines = higher scores.
Risk Bands
Quantum-NativeScore 0–10Built on NIST PQC standards from genesis. No quantum migration required.
LowScore < 30Limited cryptographic exposure or migration already well underway.
ModerateScore 30–49Real exposure but manageable migration path with sufficient lead time.
HighScore 50–64Significant exposure with complex migration requirements.
CriticalScore 65–79Severe exposure. Migration requires major coordinated effort before Q-Day.
SystemicScore 80–100Existential exposure. A breach would cause cascading failures across dependent systems.
Value at Risk Formula

VAR estimates the dollar value that could be compromised in a quantum breach scenario. It is a modelled projection — not a financial calculation.

(Market Cap + TVL × 0.5)×(Score ÷ 100)×0.65
= Estimated Value at Risk

The 0.65 discount reflects that not all at-risk capital would be extractable in a realistic attack scenario. Market cap comes from CoinGecko (live). TVL comes from DeFiLlama (live). Non-crypto entities use estimated figures — labelled "Estimated" on each card.

AI Transparency

Claude (Anthropic) is used in two places. Both are clearly labelled on every card that shows AI-generated content.

1 — Threat Narratives (entity detail view)

For the 12 pre-indexed entities, narratives are written by human researchers and served statically — no AI call is made and no cost is incurred. For all other entities, Claude Haiku generates a 2–3 paragraph plain-text assessment using the entity name, category, risk score, and cryptographic scheme. Results are cached in your browser for 7 days so the same entity is never generated twice on your device.

2 — Unknown Entity Scoring (AI Score button)

When you search for an entity not in the index, Claude Sonnet returns a structured JSON object with: name, category, cryptographic scheme, five risk factor scores (0–100 each), estimated market cap, and a 2-sentence summary. AI-scored entities show a blue banner and should be treated as informed estimates — not authoritative assessments.

Known Limitations
Q-Day timelines are speculative. Credible estimates range from 5 to 20+ years. The Doom Clock uses a single projected date for illustration only.
Factor scores for the 12 indexed entities are set by human researchers based on publicly available information. They are not independently audited.
AI-scored entities rely on Claude's training data, which has a knowledge cutoff. Recent PQC migration announcements may not be reflected.
Value at Risk figures are illustrative projections only. They are not financial estimates and must not inform investment decisions.
Wallet scan data reflects on-chain balances only. Off-chain and custodial holdings are not included.
This site does not constitute financial, security, or legal advice of any kind.
