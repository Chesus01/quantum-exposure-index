How this is calculated. Loans are rebuilt from Blend contract events on Ethereum (0x2946…a20B). Debt uses Blend's continuous interest: principal × e^(APR × years since the loan started or was last refinanced). Floors come from OpenSea. Loans in a refinancing auction can owe slightly more than shown, because the auction rate climbs over time.
Not financial advice. Floors move fast and thin collections can be mispriced. Check the loan on chain before acting.
